# Code of Conduct

## The Short Version

Be kind. Be respectful. Everyone is welcome here regardless of who they are.

## Expected Behavior

- Treat others with kindness, empathy, and respect
- Welcome people of all backgrounds and identities
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully
- Assume good intent, but prioritize impact over intent

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or threats of any kind
- Discrimination based on gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, race, ethnicity, disability, age, religion, or any other protected characteristic
- Transphobia, homophobia, sexism, racism, or other bigotry
- Deliberate intimidation, stalking, or unwelcome attention
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Sustained disruption of discussions or project spaces

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, and any context where you're representing or participating in this project.

## Enforcement

This is a small project maintained by one person. If someone violates this Code of Conduct, I will take whatever action I believe is appropriate, up to and including a permanent ban from all project spaces. There is no appeals committee or multi-step process — my judgment is final.

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, open an issue or contact me directly.

## Good Faith

Most interactions won't need this document. If you're here to contribute, learn, or play a roguelike, you're welcome. Just treat people the way you'd want to be treated.
